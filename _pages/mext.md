The Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship is offered by Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology to international students who wish to study at Japanese universities. It is one of the most generous scholarships for study in Japan, providing full tuition, monthly stipends, and round-trip travel expenses. Applicants for the undergraduate program take written exams, including math. **Math A** is for humanities and social science applicants, and **Math B** for science and engineering applicants.

Below are past math exams, along with my handwritten solutions. For current application details, see [Study in Japan](https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/) or contact the Japanese embassy in your country.
